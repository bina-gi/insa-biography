I'm Biniyam Girma

About me

I had the best time of my life during my childhood. I played a lot with my friends every day and enjoyed every moment of it. I was born on Meskerem 18, 1995, and I grew up in a big and loving family. My father's name is Girma Tefera, and my mother's name is Wederyelesh Sidelil. I have four brothers and three sisters, so our house was always full of noise, laughter, and fun. Growing up with so many siblings taught me how to share, how to be patient, and how to enjoy simple things in life. I was not a good student at first, and I was lazy with my homework. I would always find an excuse to play instead of study. But slowly, with the help of my family, friends, and teachers, I started to change. They encouraged me and pushed me to take my studies seriously, and little by little, I began to like education. Everything changed when I touched a computer for the first time. That small moment opened a new world for me and became the start of my journey into technology, a journey that I am still on today.

MY Journey

  • 2000 - 2009

    PRIMARY SCHOOL

    I spent my childhood playing with my friends and enjoying life without many worries. We played many games together, and those years still feel like the happiest time of my life. In the classroom, I was not a serious student at first. I did not like homework, and I often avoided studying. But with support and encouragement from my family and teachers, I slowly began to improve and take my education more seriously. At grade seven, everything changed when I touched a computer for the first time. I played a game called Solitaire, and it felt amazing to me. After that day, I started to spend my free time on the computer whenever I could. I began typing and editing documents in both Amharic and English, even though the computer had no internet connection at the time.

  • 2010 - 2013

    SECONDARY SCHOOL

    When I reached grade eleven, I got my first mobile phone, and it changed the way I saw technology. I was truly amazed by how mobile apps were built, and from that moment, I wanted to build one myself. Since my computer was not powerful enough to develop mobile apps, I decided to start with something simpler. I began learning HTML and CSS on my own, step by step, trying to understand how websites were structured and designed. During this same period, I also prepared for and took my university entrance exam. It was a stressful but important time, and I am proud to say that I passed it successfully.

  • 2014 - now

    UNIVERSITY

    After passing the entrance exam, I continued learning and moved on to JavaScript, which opened up many new possibilities for me. I built small projects to practice what I was learning, including a simple calculator and a number guessing game. These small projects gave me confidence and made me even more excited about technology. Soon after, I joined university, where I am currently continuing my education. Since then, I have been working hard to grow my skills, explore new tools, and build a stronger foundation in software development.

Goals

Start my own startup

My biggest goal is to start my own company one day. I am currently working as a software developer, and I see this as the first step toward building something of my own. I want to use the skills I am gaining now as a strong foundation for my future business.

Expand into hardware

Right now, I mostly work with software, but in the future, I want to expand into hardware as well. I believe combining software and hardware skills will help me build more complete and powerful solutions, and open up new opportunities for growth.

Contribute to Ethiopia

I want to use my skills to make a real difference in Ethiopia, especially in the health, education, and financial sectors. I believe technology can solve real problems in these areas, and I want to be part of that change with the solutions I build.

Build a big company

My long-term goal is to grow my startup into a big and successful company. I want it to be a place that creates value, solves real problems, and grows step by step until it becomes something big and respected.
